# Design Context for Wecun (微存)

## Users

**Personal archivists** — individuals backing up their own Weibo history or saving content from favorite creators. They use Wecun at their desk, likely on a personal computer, in focused sessions. They value their digital memories and want reliable access to their content outside Weibo's platform. They may not be technical experts but are comfortable with desktop applications.

## Brand Personality

**Three words**: 轻松、可靠、贴心

- **Tone**: Simple & friendly — approachable, unintimidating, like a helpful utility
- **Voice**: Chinese-first, clear and concise, with warm but professional language
- **Emotional goals**: Users should feel *confident* that their data is safe, *guided* through each step, and *satisfied* with the result

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual tone**: Clean and uncluttered with subtle warmth — not cold/utilitarian, not playful/cartoony
- **Theme**: Light mode default (archives are usually viewed during daytime), dark mode available via system preference
- **Colors**: Teal accent (existing oklch(0.62 0.15 185)) works well — suggests calm and reliability. Keep neutrals warm-tinted, not clinical gray.
- **Typography**: System fonts are appropriate — SF Pro Display + PingFang SC feel native to macOS/Windows. Focus on hierarchy and readability over distinctiveness.
- **Anti-reference**: Not "techy" dark mode with neon accents, not generic admin dashboard, not skeuomorphic document folder

## Design Principles

1. **Progressive clarity**: Each wizard step should reveal exactly what's needed, nothing more. Reduce cognitive load.
2. **Polished utility**: Every interaction should feel considered — micro-animations, hover states, transitions. Delight in details.
3. **Trust through transparency**: Show what's happening. Progress indicators, clear status messages, visible logs during long operations.
4. **Respectful of content**: The user's Weibo posts are memories. Present them with care, not as raw data dumps.
5. **Forgiving**: Easy to undo, easy to go back, clear indication of what will happen before it does.
